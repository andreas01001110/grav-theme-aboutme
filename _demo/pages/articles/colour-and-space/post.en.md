Colour works before we name it. A large red field makes a room warmer and smaller, a cool blue makes it wider and calmer.

## Three observations from the studio

1. **Size beats quantity.** One large painting has more impact than five small ones.
2. **Light decides.** The same colour looks different in daylight and under artificial light.
3. **Emptiness belongs.** White space around a painting is not wasted space.

In the seminar “Colour and Space” students test this in real rooms: with colour charts, light and a lot of patience.
