When Jon Doe took over the empty printing works on Hafenstrasse in 2018, two printing presses were still standing there. Today the front is the exhibition space and the studio is at the back.

## Bright, high, honest

The walls were left raw and the floor is the old industrial screed. Skylights provide even daylight that does not distort the colours of the paintings.

## Three exhibitions a year

Alongside his own series, the gallery shows guest positions by young artists. Every exhibition opens with a talk, often with music.

> A good space steps back and lets the paintings speak.
