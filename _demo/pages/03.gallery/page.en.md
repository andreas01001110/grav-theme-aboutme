Galerie Doe presents three exhibitions a year: his own series and guest positions by young artists. Between exhibitions the studio is open to visitors.

## Programme

- **Exhibitions:** three a year, six to eight weeks each
- **Open studio:** every first Saturday of the month
- **Talks:** artist talks and guided tours on request
- **Education:** workshops for school classes and groups

## Opening hours

Thursday to Saturday, 2 to 7 pm, and by appointment.
